How this is used

Since July 8, 2026, importers of CPSC-regulated products must eFile certificate data through CBP's ACE system before goods clear customs. Send this CSV to your customs broker — they map these fields into the PGA message set on the entry filing. This prototype generates the data format; your broker (or you as importer of record) remains the filer. Not legal advice.

Honest disclaimer. CertVault (working title — trademark check required before launch) is a prototype built for validation. It is not legal advice, not a law firm, not a testing laboratory, and not a customs broker. Sample certificates, labs, dates, and SKUs are fictional demo data. Always confirm regulatory requirements with the CPSC Small Business Ombudsman, your testing lab, your customs broker, or qualified counsel before importing or selling regulated products.
